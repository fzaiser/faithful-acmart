\section*{Components}
An event with a venue and no title of its own is the parentheses alone \cite{EvVenue}, and a subtitle or an addon prints without a title beside it \cite{TiSubOnly,TiAddOnly}.
A main title takes a period where no volume follows it and a colon where one does \cite{MtPlain,MtVol}, an addon rides with it \cite{MtAddon}, and a title equal to it is printed once \cite{MtSame}.
A language field is a list \cite{LgThree}, an event the numeric guard cannot read a date for is skipped \cite{EvGuard}, a series that ends in a dot keeps the next separator out \cite{PnSerNum}, and an addon behind a colon takes a space \cite{AdColon}.
A title that ends in a colon takes a space where another takes a period \cite{KoTitle,KoSub,KoBook,KoMain}, and an organization is a list \cite{OrThree}.
