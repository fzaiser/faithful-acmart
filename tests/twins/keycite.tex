\section{Introduction}
Native Typst citations \cite{Cohen07}, \cite{Kosiur01}, and \cite{Harel78} route through the bst engine.
Variants: \citet{Harel78} \citealt{Harel78} \citeyearpar{Harel78} \shortcite{Harel78} and \cite[p.~5]{Harel78}.
The bare-key shorthand carries the same postnote: \cite[p.~5]{Harel78}.
Typst's citation forms reach the same natbib commands: \citet{Harel78}, \citeauthor{Harel78}, \citeyear{Harel78}, and a silent \nocite{Cohen07}.

Postnotes: \citet[p.~5]{Harel78}, \citealt[p.~5]{Harel78}, \citeyearpar[p.~5]{Harel78}, and across two keys \citet[p.~5]{Cohen07,Harel78}.
In numbers mode natbib drops a postnote from the author and year forms: \citeauthor[p.~5]{Harel78} and \citeyear[p.~5]{Harel78}.
